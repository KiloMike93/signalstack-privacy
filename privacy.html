SignalStack Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Introduction

SignalStack provides deterministic, on-device data analysis for business and personal use. Supported user-selected data is processed locally to provide analysis, insights, evidence and decision support. SignalStack does not require imported datasets to be uploaded to SignalStack-operated servers for its core analysis.

2. Data You Choose to Import

Selected files may contain sales, orders, revenue, costs, expenses, transactions, customer or product identifiers, dates, descriptions, bank-statement or personal financial information. SignalStack accesses this information when you deliberately choose a file. It does not automatically search your files for business or financial information.

3. Processing

Core analysis is performed locally on your device using programmed rules and mathematical calculations. Imported datasets are not required to be transmitted to SignalStack servers or third-party analysis services for this core analysis.

4. Sharing and Advertising

SignalStack does not sell user-imported data, share imported datasets with advertisers or data brokers for advertising, profiling or marketing, or create advertising profiles from imported datasets.

5. Local Storage

SignalStack may store preferences, acknowledgements, analysis history, actions, outcomes, categorisation information and other application state locally on your device. Clearing app data, uninstalling SignalStack or resetting the device may remove locally stored information.

6. Retention and Deletion

SignalStack does not maintain a remote account containing copies of imported datasets as part of its core analysis service. Locally stored information can be removed using available app functionality, by clearing SignalStack app data, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Security

SignalStack is designed to minimise unnecessary movement of sensitive information. No software or device can be guaranteed completely secure; users should maintain appropriate device security and operating-system updates.

8. Device File Access

SignalStack may access files you specifically choose to analyse. File access supports the application's stated functionality and is not used for advertising or unrelated tracking.

9. Internet and Third Parties

The deterministic analysis engine is designed to operate without an internet connection for supported imported datasets and does not require third-party AI services for core mathematical analysis. Android, Google Play services or other independently installed software may communicate externally under their own privacy practices.

10. Children

SignalStack is a business and personal decision-support application and is not specifically directed at children.

11. Decision Support

Outputs are analytical decision support, not professional financial, accounting, tax, investment or legal advice.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated as SignalStack develops. Material changes to data-handling practices will be reflected in an updated policy and appropriate disclosures where required.
